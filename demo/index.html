How this page works ?

Your supplier was approved last month. That should not approve today's payment. Each card is one purchase order: its money stays locked in the contract and a milestone pays only when the buyer approved it, it is due, and a fresh signed register record passed the order's rules. The agent fetches those records on its own and sends the transactions under Agent activity; this page only reads.

StateWhat it meansWhat you can do
Payment permit
The contract's go-ahead to pay one milestone. A record that passes every rule gives one. It is valid for the order's permit time (in each card's Details; 30 min on the demo orders) from the record's signed time, and the payment uses it up. A tier never extends it.
Signed register record
A register answer (Companies House or GLEIF) signed by its Airnode, which the agent buys over x402 for $0.01. The contract takes it only from the expected signer, for the order's request, while it is fresh (Contract details: max age), and only once.
Airnode
API3's signer for one register. The contract trusts one per register (Contract details: trusted signers).
Rules
The register status check always runs; per order the buyer can add accounts filed, confirmation statement filed or LEI registration issued.
Supplier-wide stop
A failed register status on any order stops payments on every order of that supplier. The agent then holds the others without paying for a new check (contract call applySupplierFailure).
Grace period, no-data exit
After a hold the buyer can refund at once, and anyone can once the grace period passed. When an approved milestone is overdue and no valid record came within the grace period (the no-data exit), anyone can send the rest back to the buyer. The money only ever goes to the buyer.
Verification history
Standard (0 to 2 clean checks in a row), Trusted (3 to 5), Premium (6 or more). Only the register status counts, at most once per tier gap; a failed status resets it to 0. A tier only changes how often the agent re-checks between due dates. It is not a credit score.
Tokens
Digest, LEI
A digest is the hash of a signed record (request, time, data); each is accepted once. An LEI is the Legal Entity Identifier that GLEIF registers.

Reading the contract…

A signature proves the register said it, not that the supplier will deliver. Delivery stays the buyer's approval: each milestone needs both a valid permit from a fresh signed record and the buyer's approval. Signed records come from API3 AirnodeHub (Companies House, GLEIF); the contract checks signer, request, freshness and replay before acting. The verification history (Standard, Trusted, Premium) counts register checks; it is not a credit score.
