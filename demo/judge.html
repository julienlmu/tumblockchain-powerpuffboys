Try to break it

Tamper with a signed register record and see whether the live SupplierPulse+ contract would act on it. Every verdict is the contract's own: an eth_call of submitEvidence from 0x…dEaD. No wallet, no gas.

How this page works
  1. Pick an order. The contract builds the request it expects from the order's supplier and trusts one signer per registry.
  2. Load a signed record of that supplier: a sample (old on purpose), your own file from fetch-evidence.sh, or Fresh local record on the local chain. It must be at most 300 s old, or every attack stops at the freshness check.
  3. Run the full tamper test. Each attempt is an eth_call simulation, so nothing changes on chain. It passes when the valid record is accepted and every attack is rejected.
  4. Replay last. Send the valid record for real, then replay it: the contract answers Replayed.
